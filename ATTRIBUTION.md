## Minecraft (Mojang Studios)

The dungeons are built from Mojang's own trial-chamber structure templates, template
pools and loot tables, and every item, entity and effect id is checked against the game's
registry. `scripts/fetch_vanilla.py` extracts all of this from the official Minecraft 26.2
server jar on your own machine, into `vendor/vanilla-26.2/`. It is used under the
[Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/eula) and never redistributed by this repository.
